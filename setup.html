1 · Point at your export

Pick the folder you unzipped your Twitter/X archive into (the one with Your archive.html). The wizard reads its data/manifest.js to find your group chats, every part file, the headers and the media. Click Browse… (a native picker on Windows) or paste the path.

or pick the three files yourself

Picking them one by one? All three are required. They're ignored while an archive folder is filled in.

Big archives split this into -part1, -part2… files. Pick the first one; the other parts in the same folder are added automatically.

Browse… open any file inside the media folder, we will use its folder.

      ✓ Archive already built. Downloaded a newer Twitter/X archive? Add a newer export merges its new messages in — your names, photos, removed people and group chats stay as they are. To begin again from nothing, use Start over at the bottom of the page.
      Adding a newer export. Pick it above, check it, then merge. Messages already in your archive are kept and new ones are added; the archive as it is now is kept as data.js.bak.

      2 · Name the group optional

      Restore the group's real name and photo. The photo becomes the sidebar brand mark. You can change these later in Settings.

      3 · Name everyone optional

      The export only has numeric IDs. Use each person's sample messages & shared media (Twitter/X links excluded — they don't help) to recognize them. Add a name, a photo, and mark which one is you.

      4 · Finish

      Your archive is ready

      Save your names, photos and "you" marker, then open the archive.

      Erase all setup data?

      This deletes everything the wizard created in personal_data/ — the built archive, names, photos, and the copied media & source files — so you can start setup from a clean slate. This cannot be undone.

      Type RESET to confirm:

      Remove from your archive?

      This hides them from your archive; it doesn't delete them. They are kept in a private folder on this computer (personal_data/.removed/) until you un-remove them, which brings them back, or until Start over erases everything.
